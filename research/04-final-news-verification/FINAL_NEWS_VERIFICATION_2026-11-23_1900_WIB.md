## 2. Date and time qualification

### 2.1 Global date window

The project rule allows an event or its first report to qualify if its timestamp falls on 22 November in at least one civil time zone, from UTC−12 through UTC+14.

The corresponding UTC interval is:

**2026-11-21 10:00 UTC (inclusive) to 2026-11-23 12:00 UTC (exclusive).**

- Earliest boundary: 00:00 on 22 November in UTC+14.
- Latest boundary: 00:00 on 23 November in UTC−12.
- At 19:00 WIB on 23 November, the UTC−12 zone has just reached midnight on 23 November. This is the last possible cutoff for a timestamp to still fall on 22 November somewhere in the world.

### 2.2 Important distinction

A date-only article label is not sufficient for a cross-zone-only candidate. The report must have a defensible timestamp, and the earliest known report should be used rather than a later syndicated copy.

Three categories are used:

- **QUALIFIED:** event or first report is supported within the global date window.
- **CROSS-ZONE / CONDITIONAL:** only qualifies in a limited timezone or only if an unresolved timestamp is confirmed.
- **NOT QUALIFIED / EXCLUDE:** the available event and earliest-report timestamps do not meet the date rule, or the evidence is too weak to admit the story.

### 2.3 23:00 WIB delta check

The 23:00 WIB check is still useful for late corrections, updated casualty counts, new official statements, and source conflicts.

However, publication timestamps at 23:00 WIB are already outside the 22 November date window in every timezone. A late report can still support a story if the **event itself** is already inside the eligible window; a late publication timestamp cannot independently make a 21 November event eligible.
